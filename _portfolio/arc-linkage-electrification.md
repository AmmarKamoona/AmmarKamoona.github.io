ARC Linkage LP230100439 ($150K, 2023) on AI for electrification, with AGL, AusNet, and SpendWatt as industry partners. Role: Investigator.

The project develops machine learning for household electrification: EV charging detection from smart-meter data, PV generation forecasting, and residential demand prediction. Outputs feed into distribution-network planning and retailer-side tariff and product design. Related papers include the memory-augmented transformer for EV charging detection (*Applied Energy*, 2025), residential load profiling under EV adoption (*SEGN*, 2026), and PV/demand forecasting work in *Energy Reports* (2023).
