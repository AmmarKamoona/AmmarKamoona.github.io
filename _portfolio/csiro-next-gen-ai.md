CSIRO Next Generation AI Graduates Program ($1.4M, 2023) on AI for energy systems. Role: Investigator.

The program funds PhD and early-career researchers working on AI applied to the Australian energy transition. My contribution focuses on temporal learning for load and generation forecasting, and on transferring these methods into industry pilots with our partners.
