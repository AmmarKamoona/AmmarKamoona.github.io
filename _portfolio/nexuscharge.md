NexusCharge is a $4M Australian Economic Accelerator project (2024) that translates machine learning research into a commercial intelligent EV charging product. My role is Collaborative Entrepreneur and ML lead.

The system learns household charging patterns from smart-meter and charger telemetry and coordinates charging schedules to reduce peak network stress and charging cost while respecting user constraints. The technical stack combines demand forecasting, event detection (online EV charging identification), and optimisation-based scheduling.

Industry partner: ABB Australia. Related publication: *Online electric vehicle charging detection based on a memory-augmented transformer architecture* (Applied Energy, 2025).
