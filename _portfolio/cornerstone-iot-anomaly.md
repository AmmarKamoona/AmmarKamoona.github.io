AI and Embedded Systems Engineer at Cornerstone Solutions Pty Ltd (2018–2019). Designed and deployed embedded AI systems for construction site safety.

The devices run anomaly detection models on low-power microcontrollers close to the sensor, so unsafe events are flagged in real time without depending on a cloud round-trip. This work formed the basis of my RMIT IoT Research Forum presentation (2019).
