Hello, I am a Research Fellow in AI and Machine Learning at RMIT University in Melbourne, Australia, and an AI Scientist / Computer Vision Engineer at Powercor Australia. I obtained my PhD from RMIT University in 2022 under the supervision of Prof. Alireza Bab-Hadiashar and Prof. Reza Hoseinnezhad. My research focuses on machine learning and computer vision, including deep anomaly detection, transformer-based temporal learning, Vision Language Models, Bayesian deep learning, information theory, and Random Finite Set theory with applications to 3D perception, image classification, and object detection.

In addition to my research, I have five years of university teaching experience in AI, machine learning, and electrical engineering. At RMIT University I have taught Intelligent Systems, Modelling and Simulation of Engineering Systems, and Digital Fundamentals. I also served as a Lecturer in Electrical Engineering at the University of Kufa in Iraq. I am an active reviewer for top-tier journals such as Expert Systems with Applications, Applied Energy, and Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence.

I am passionate about using theoretical research to build practical solutions for industry problems. As a lifelong learner, I keep looking for new ideas that sharpen my understanding of ML and CV. When I am not working, I enjoy reading, jogging, and a good cup of coffee. Thank you for taking the time to learn a little about me.
